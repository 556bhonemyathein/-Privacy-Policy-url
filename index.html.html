Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pocket Pilot ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. We built Pocket Pilot to work entirely on your device, with your privacy as a priority.

Information We Collect

Pocket Pilot does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All information you enter into the App — including account details, transactions, categories, balances, and reports — is stored locally on your device only.

Data Storage

Because all data stays on your device:

  • We do not have access to your financial information, transactions, or account details.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will permanently delete all information stored by the App.

Third-Party Services

Pocket Pilot does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No usage data is sent off your device.

Permissions

Any device permissions requested by the App (such as storage access, if used for backups) are used solely to provide App functionality and are never used to collect or transmit personal data externally.

Children's Privacy

Pocket Pilot does not knowingly collect information from children, as no information is collected from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
